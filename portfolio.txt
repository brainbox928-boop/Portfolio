Sector-Specific Pages or Case StudiesConsider adding three distinct case study deep-dives or sections on your site to cater to your three target audiences:🚀 Freelance Pitch: Focus on rapid deployment, clean UI/UX, and mobile responsiveness. Show that you can take a design from Figma to a live, pixel-perfect site independently.


🏢 Private Tech Pitch: Focus on your architecture. Mention Git workflows, Agile methodologies, performance optimization, and how your Marketplace handles complex data flows.



📜 Government/Public Sector Pitch: Dedicate a section or bullet points to Web Accessibility (WCAG), semantic HTML, cross-browser compatibility, and secure front-end practices. Government tech stack requirements often value stability and inclusivity over the trendiest frameworks.
