Northline Finance

A front-end prototype for exploring personal and business financing, estimating payments, and walking through an application flow.

Challenge

Make a complex financial-services journey easier to understand before an applicant begins a multi-step application.

My Work

Built the static frontend across personal and business financing paths, including application, status, FAQ, and supporting information pages.

Implementation

Used Bootstrap 5 and vanilla JavaScript to connect the loan calculator and application experience. The displayed estimates and application, upload, status, and admin data are mock/demo data.

Outcome

A deployed, navigable financing prototype for exploring the workflow. No lending, approval, conversion, or business-impact metrics are available.

Technologies: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, vanilla JavaScript

Prototype limitation: Applications and uploads are not submitted to a real lender. Do not enter real financial, identity, or other sensitive information. Brand, rates, terms, licensing, and legal content are placeholders.
